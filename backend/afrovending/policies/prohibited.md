## 1. Purpose
AfroVending is a U.S. marketplace for African, Caribbean and diaspora-inspired products sold by independent Vendors. This Policy lists products that may **never** be sold, and products that may be sold **only with prior approval and proof of compliance**. Vendors must accept this Policy before they are approved to sell, and again when a material change is made. It forms part of the Vendor Agreement.

Vendors are responsible for knowing and following all laws that apply to their products — federal, state, local and, for imports, foreign export and U.S. import law. If you're unsure about a product, ask info@afrovending.com before listing it.

## 2. Prohibited — never allowed
1. **Illegal products,** and anything whose sale, possession or shipment is unlawful in the U.S. or the destination state.
2. **Counterfeit or replica goods** and unauthorized copies of trademarked products or designs. This includes fake designer, sports or entertainment merchandise.
3. **Intellectual-property violations:** pirated media, software or digital files, and items infringing copyrights, trademarks, patents or rights of publicity.
4. **Stolen goods,** or goods without lawful title.
5. **Weapons:**
   - firearms, ammunition, firearm parts and kits;
   - explosives and fireworks;
   - silencers;
   - switchblades and other knives that are illegal where sold or shipped;
   - weapons designed to cause harm, including stun guns where they are restricted.
6. **Drugs and drug paraphernalia:**
   - controlled substances and illegal drugs;
   - prescription medicines;
   - marijuana and THC products;
   - products marketed for intoxication;
   - drug paraphernalia.
7. **Tobacco, nicotine and vaping products,** including e-cigarettes and e-liquids. [PAYMENT-PROVIDER REVIEW]
8. **Alcohol.** Alcohol cannot be sold without the required licences, and the Marketplace does not currently support licensed alcohol sales. [PAYMENT-PROVIDER REVIEW]
9. **Hazardous materials:** dangerous goods that can't lawfully be shipped by ordinary carriers, such as flammable, toxic, corrosive or radioactive materials and pressurised containers.
10. **Recalled products,** and products banned by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission or other agencies.
11. **Unsafe products for children:** children's products without the required testing or certification (such as a Children's Product Certificate), and items with small parts or lead hazards.
12. **Wildlife and animal products:**
    - ivory, endangered or protected species and their parts;
    - live animals;
    - products restricted under CITES, the U.S. Endangered Species Act or the Lacey Act.
13. **Human remains and body parts,** except hair products lawfully sold for cosmetic use.
14. **Medical claims and devices:** products claiming to cure, treat or prevent disease without approval, and medical devices that need FDA clearance.
15. **Adult and sexually explicit content.** [PAYMENT-PROVIDER REVIEW]
16. **Hateful or violent content:**
    - items that promote hatred, violence or discrimination;
    - Nazi or terrorist propaganda;
    - items glorifying violent crimes.
17. **Financial and other restricted items:**
    - currency, counterfeit money, stamps and coins sold above face value as investments;
    - securities, gift cards from third parties, lottery tickets and gambling services;
    - cryptocurrencies.

18. **Personal data and accounts:** personal information, account credentials and government IDs or documents.
19. **Services and digital goods,** unless AfroVending enables them in future.
20. **Prohibited by Stripe:** anything Stripe prohibits under its Restricted Businesses list or Services Agreement.

## 4. Listing standards
- Listings must be accurate and must not make false claims about origin, materials, health benefits or authenticity.
- Do not use other brands' trademarks to attract buyers.
- Do not list a prohibited item under a different name or category.

## 5. Enforcement
1. **Detection.** Every new product is reviewed before going live. AfroVending may also act on reports from customers, rights owners, payment providers or authorities.
2. **Listing removal.** Listings that break this Policy are rejected or removed, and the Vendor is notified with the reason.
3. **Repeat or serious violations** may lead to account restrictions, suspension or termination, held earnings (as described in the Vendor Agreement) and removal of any Founding Vendor badge.
4. **Legal escalation.** Illegal activity may be reported to law enforcement or regulators, and payment-provider requirements may be applied.
5. **Appeals.** A Vendor may appeal a decision within [14] days by emailing info@afrovending.com with supporting evidence. A different reviewer will consider the appeal where possible.
6. **Records.** Enforcement actions are recorded in AfroVending's administrator audit log.
7. **Rights-owner reports.** Owners of intellectual-property rights can report infringing listings to info@afrovending.com.

## 6. Changes
We may update this Policy as laws or payment-provider rules change. If a change is material, Vendors must accept the new version before they can publish new products or accept new orders.
