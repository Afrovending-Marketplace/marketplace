## 1. Overview
Products on AfroVending are sold by independent Vendors. This Policy sets the minimum return and refund standard every Vendor must follow. A Vendor may offer more generous terms on its store or listings, but not less generous ones.

## 2. Cancelling before the Vendor accepts
You can cancel a Vendor's part of your order from your order page until the Vendor accepts it. You will receive a full refund for that part, including its shipping and tax.

## 3. Returns
1. **Return window.** You may request a return within **14 days** after your item is delivered or picked up.
2. **How to request.** Open your order, choose the items and quantities, and give a reason. Photos help for damaged, defective or incorrect items.
3. **Vendor response.** The Vendor must respond within **48 hours**. They will approve the return (with return instructions), offer a solution or decline it with a reason. If the Vendor doesn't respond in time, or you disagree with the decision, the request is escalated to AfroVending for review.
4. **Non-returnable items** — unless they arrive damaged, defective or not as described:
   - perishable food;
   - opened cosmetics and personal-care items;
   - custom or made-to-order items;
   - gift cards;
   - items the listing clearly marks as final sale.

5. **Condition.** Items should be returned unused and in their original packaging, unless they are damaged or defective.
6. **Return shipping.** If the item is damaged, defective, wrong or not as described, the Vendor covers return shipping or offers a refund without requiring the item back. For change-of-mind returns, [DECIDE: customer pays return shipping unless the Vendor states otherwise].

## 4. Refunds
1. Refunds are issued through AfroVending to **your original payment method** via Stripe. You'll see the refund on your order page and get an email.
2. **Timing.** Card issuers usually take 5–10 business days to show a refund. [CONFIRM with Stripe.]
3. **Partial refunds.** A refund may cover some items or units, part of the price (for example, for a minor defect you agree to keep) or shipping. **Refunded items that haven't shipped yet are cancelled and won't be sent.**
4. **What's included.** Refunds include the item price and the tax charged on it. Original shipping is refunded when the whole Vendor order is cancelled, or when the return is due to a Vendor error. [CONFIRM.]
5. **Promotions.** If you used a coupon, the refund reflects the discounted price you paid.

## 5. Damaged, defective or incorrect items
Report the problem within [3] days of delivery, with photos, through a return request. You may receive a replacement, a refund or another remedy agreed with the Vendor.

## 6. Disputes and chargebacks
- If you can't resolve an issue with the Vendor, AfroVending will review it and may issue a refund under this Policy.
- Filing a card dispute (chargeback) while a return or refund is in progress may delay it. Please contact us first at info@afrovending.com.
- Abuse of returns or chargebacks may lead to account restrictions.
