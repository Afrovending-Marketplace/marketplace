## 1. Who we are
AfroVending Online Marketplace LLC ("AfroVending", "we") operates the afrovending.com marketplace. It is based at [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. For privacy questions or requests, contact **info@afrovending.com**.

This Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to shoppers, Vendors and visitors. Vendors that receive customer information to fulfill orders are responsible for their own handling of it, as described in the Vendor Agreement.

## 2. Information we collect
**You give us:**
- **Account details:** your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), plus your marketing preference.
- **Addresses and order details:** shipping addresses, the items you order, delivery or pickup choices, pickup appointments, cancellation and return requests, and the reasons you give.
- **Messages and reviews:** order messages with Vendors, support tickets, contact-form messages, and product and seller reviews.
- **Vendor applications:** business name, contact details, location, store information and the categories you sell in. We may also request verification documents, and we keep a record of which policies you accepted.
- **Privacy requests** that you submit.

**Collected automatically:**
- **Security and technical data:** IP address, browser type, sign-in events and timestamps. We use this to keep accounts secure — for example, to limit repeated failed sign-ins — to prevent fraud, and to diagnose errors.
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, searches and cart activity. Analytics data is collected only if you allow analytics in your cookie preferences. See the Cookie Policy.
- **Error reports:** technical details when a page or request fails. We remove passwords, tokens, payment details and email addresses before storing them.

**From others:**
- **Stripe** (our payment processor) tells us whether a payment succeeded, the card brand and last four digits, and refund or dispute status. **We never receive or store full card numbers.**
- **Referrals:** if you arrive through a referral link.

## 3. How we use information
We use your information to:
- provide the Marketplace: accounts, carts, checkout, orders, payments, refunds, pickup, messaging and support;
- send service messages, such as order confirmations, shipping and pickup updates, password resets and security alerts;
- review Vendor applications, enforce our policies, prevent fraud and abuse, and protect users;
- calculate fees, commissions, taxes and payouts, and keep accurate financial records;
- improve the Marketplace (using analytics only with your consent);
- send marketing emails **only if you have opted in**. These include wishlist price-drop and back-in-stock summaries and saved-cart reminders. You can unsubscribe at any time;
- comply with law and respond to lawful requests.
