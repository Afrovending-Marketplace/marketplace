## 1. What this Policy covers
This Policy explains how afrovending.com uses cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, and how you can control them. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

## 2. What cookies are
Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature. Both help websites remember information between pages and visits.

## 4. Your choices
- **Cookie banner:** on your first visit you can accept all cookies, reject the optional ones, or manage your preferences individually.
- **Changing your mind:** you can change your choices at any time from **Cookie Settings** in the site footer. We keep your choices until you change them.
- **Browser controls:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser, but essential features such as sign-in and the cart may then stop working.
