## 1. Who ships your order
Each product on AfroVending is sold and shipped by an independent Vendor, not by AfroVending. If your order contains products from several Vendors, each Vendor's items arrive separately, and each has its own shipping charge, tracking and timing.

## 2. Where we deliver
We currently deliver only to addresses in the **United States**. [CONFIRM: territories, APO/FPO and PO boxes.] Some Vendors may limit where they ship, or offer local pickup only.

## 3. Shipping options and costs
- Each Vendor sets its own shipping options and prices. You choose an option for each Vendor at checkout. Shipping costs and any applicable sales tax are shown before you pay.
- Some Vendors offer free shipping or local pickup.

## 4. Handling time
Handling time is the time a Vendor needs to prepare your order before it ships or is ready for pickup.
- Each shipping or pickup option shows its handling time, counted in business days. Weekends and Marketplace non-business days are excluded. The default is 3 business days.
- The handling deadline is set when the Vendor accepts your order.
- If a Vendor is running late, we remind them, and you may be notified. Overdue orders may be escalated to AfroVending.

Delivery estimates from carriers are not guaranteed.

## 5. Order acceptance and cancellation
After payment, each Vendor accepts your part of the order. Until the Vendor accepts it, you can cancel that part from your order page for a full refund. After acceptance, contact the Vendor; see the Returns & Refunds Policy.

## 6. Tracking and partial shipments
- When a Vendor ships, they add a carrier and tracking number where available, and you can follow the shipment from your order page.
- Vendors may ship part of an order first. Any items that can't be shipped may be cancelled and refunded.

## 7. Local pickup
- **Pickup details:** if a Vendor offers local pickup, the pickup location, hours, closures and instructions are shown at checkout and on your order.
- **Booking a pickup time:** once your order is marked ready, you can book a pickup appointment from the Vendor's available slots, within the next 14 days and during opening hours. You can also reschedule or cancel the appointment.
- **At pickup:** bring your order number. The Vendor may ask for identification.
- **Uncollected orders:** these may be handled under the Returns & Refunds Policy. [CONFIRM: what happens to uncollected pickup orders and after how long.]

## 8. Delivery problems
- **Lost or late shipments:** first contact the Vendor through order messages. If tracking shows no movement for [X] business days past the estimate, or the parcel is marked delivered but you didn't receive it, you may request help, and unresolved issues escalate to AfroVending.
- **Damaged or wrong items:** report them within [3] days of delivery, with photos, through a return request. See the Returns & Refunds Policy.
- **Wrong address:** check your address carefully, because Vendors ship to the address you enter at checkout. AfroVending and Vendors are not responsible for orders sent to an incorrect address you provided.

## 9. Perishable, fragile and regulated items
Vendors selling food, cosmetics or fragile goods must package and ship them appropriately and comply with the Prohibited Products Policy. Some products may have shipping restrictions.
