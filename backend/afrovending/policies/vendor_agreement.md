## 2. Eligibility and onboarding
1. To sell, you must:
   - be at least 18;
   - be legally able to enter contracts;
   - be located in, and sell to customers in, the **United States** (currently the only supported market).
2. You must apply with accurate business information and verify your email address. AfroVending may request verification documents.
3. AfroVending reviews every application. We may approve it, ask for more information or decline it. Approval is at our discretion.
4. You must accept the current Vendor Agreement and Prohibited Products Policy before approval. You must also accept later versions where we mark a change as material.
5. **Founding Vendors:** vendors approved before or during the launch period may receive a "Founding Vendor" badge. The badge is recognition only. It does not change fees, search ranking or other terms.

## 3. Your store and listings
You are responsible for:
- **accurate listings:** truthful titles, descriptions, photos, materials, sizes, ingredients, allergens, origin claims, prices, variants and stock levels;
- **lawful products:** every product must comply with applicable federal, state and local law — including labelling, safety, food, cosmetics and import rules — and with the Prohibited Products Policy;
- **your rights:** you hold all rights to the content and brands you upload;
- **keeping stock accurate:** you must not accept orders you can't fulfill.

All new products are reviewed by AfroVending before they go live. We may reject, remove or suspend listings, and every enforcement action is recorded in our audit log.

## 4. Orders and fulfillment
1. Customers pay AfroVending through Stripe at checkout. You receive an order notification for your part of the order (your "vendor order").
2. You must accept or decline a new vendor order promptly. A customer may cancel your part before you accept it.
3. Ship or prepare orders within the handling time you set for each shipping or pickup option. The default is 3 business days. AfroVending sets the minimum and maximum. Late orders trigger reminders and may be escalated to AfroVending.
4. Add tracking for shipped orders where available. Partial shipments are allowed.
5. **Local pickup:** keep your pickup hours, closures and appointment slots up to date, and mark orders ready only when they are ready.
6. Respond to customer messages promptly and professionally, and only through the Marketplace. Do not move transactions or payments off the Marketplace.

## 5. Returns, refunds and disputes
1. You must honour the Returns & Refunds Policy. You may offer more generous terms, but not less generous ones.
2. You must respond to return requests within the response time AfroVending sets, currently **48 hours**. Unanswered or disputed requests escalate to AfroVending, and our decision is binding for Marketplace purposes.
3. **Refunds are issued through AfroVending** to the customer's original payment method. If you refund units that haven't shipped, those units are cancelled and must not be shipped.
4. Refunds, chargebacks and reversals reduce your earnings, as described in Section 7.
